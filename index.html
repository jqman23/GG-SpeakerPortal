Getting Started

What is this Speaker Resource & Questionnaire Page?

This page is your preparation hub for the 2026 Global Gathering. It includes the Speaker Questionnaire (now closed), a session lookup tool, FAQs, Attendee Hub guidance, and other presenter resources.

How is the 2026 Global Gathering structured?

A Global Gathering for the Future of Child Welfare is a three-day virtual convening taking place October 6–8, 2026 (MDT).

Day 1 features Skill Building Institutes, which are longer, hands-on learning experiences. Days 2 and 3 include keynotes, workshops, strategy sessions, international exchanges, and creative spaces.

As a speaker, what do I need to do to prepare for the event?

First, make sure you’re registered for the Global Gathering. Speakers must be registered to access Attendee Hub. If you aren’t registered yet, register here. Allow 15 minutes after registration before signing in with your registration email and verification code.

Then explore Attendee Hub, review your session information, coordinate with your co-presenters, and read the sign-in and hosting instructions before your session.

What is the Speaker Questionnaire?

The Speaker Questionnaire was the required session-specific form that one presenter from each session needed to complete. The form confirmed key details about each presentation.

The Questionnaire is now closed. The form was session-specific, so the questions changed depending on the session selected. Only one presenter from each session needed to complete this form.

Profiles & Session Details

How do I know if my session is in the Embedded or regular Zoom format?

Check your Attendee Hub video format using the Session Information Lookup tool on this Speaker Resource Page.

How do I know if my session is CEU Eligible?

Check your session using the Session Information Lookup tool on this Speaker Resource Page. You can also locate your session in Attendee Hub and look for the label “CEU Eligible” or “Not CEU Eligible.”

How do I edit my speaker or session profile, or report information that does not look right?

You can update your speaker profile—such as your name, company, title, bio, and social media links—and session details—such as the title and description—at any time in the Speaker Portal. Once logged in, select My Profile or My Sessions at the top of the page.

If a speaker needs to be added to or removed from your session, contact the Global Gathering Team at globalgathering@cuanschutz.edu.

My session type or duration looks different from what I originally submitted. Is that correct?

Likely, yes. As we finalized the 2026 Global Gathering program, some sessions were adjusted in session type or duration to better fit the overall schedule and programming structure.

The Session Information Lookup reflects the most current information we have on file for your session.

If you have questions about your session type or duration, or would like more context about any changes, please reach out to us at globalgathering@cuanschutz.edu. We're happy to walk through the details with you.

If my session is CEU-eligible, what do I need to submit?

For CEU-eligible sessions, 3 measurable learning objectives and 3 knowledge-check questions were requested through the Speaker Questionnaire. The Questionnaire is now closed. If you have questions about your CEU materials, please contact the Global Gathering Team at globalgathering@cuanschutz.edu.

Will my session be recorded?

To see if your session is set to be recorded, please use the Session Information Lookup tab. If you have questions about your recording status, please contact the Global Gathering Team at globalgathering@cuanschutz.edu.

Session Format & Features

What is the difference between a Zoom and Embedded session?

Zoom sessions use a standard Zoom-based setup connected to the virtual event experience. Embedded sessions are built more directly into Attendee Hub, the online event platform. You can view the differences by clicking here.

Can I use polls during my session?

Yes. Whether your session is Embedded or on Zoom, you will be able to create and launch polls. Please review the Speaker Guide (PDF) for instructions for your format.

How do I generate a transcript for my session?

Zoom sessions: In the Zoom meeting toolbar, select Transcript—it may be under More. At the end of your session, select Save Transcript to save the file to your computer.

Embedded sessions: Unfortunately, transcripts aren’t available for embedded sessions.

How do I save the notes from my session?

Zoom sessions: Open Notes in the Zoom meeting—it may be under More. Highlight all comments, right-click, and select Copy. Then paste the text into a document, email, or wherever you keep your records.

Embedded sessions: Open the notes panel, highlight all comments, right-click, and select Copy. Then paste the text into a document, email, or wherever you keep your records.

How do I use the waiting room in my session?

Zoom sessions: In the Zoom meeting toolbar, select Host tools—it may be under More—then select Enable waiting room. Attendees wait there until you admit them.

Embedded sessions: Embedded sessions do not have a waiting room. Attendees can join the session starting 5 minutes before it begins.

How do I change my name from “Session Host”?

Zoom sessions: You join with the default name Session Host. Open Participants in the Zoom meeting toolbar, hover over your name, then select More → Rename. Type the name you want attendees to see and confirm. Do this at the start of your session, and repeat it if you leave and rejoin.

What are Skill Building Institutes?

Skill Building Institutes are extended, hands-on learning sessions offered on Day 1 of the Global Gathering. They are designed for deeper practice, applied tools, and skill development.

Skill Building Institute presenters will receive additional information from the Global Gathering Team as the event nears.

Attendee Hub & Hosting

What is Attendee Hub, and how do I log in?

Attendee Hub is the online event center for the Global Gathering. For Zoom and Embedded sessions, presenters use it to access and host their sessions. If you are viewing this page within Attendee Hub, you are already logged in. Otherwise, open Attendee Hub, enter your name and registration email, and enter the verification code sent to your email or phone.

How do I start my Zoom or Embedded session?

Hover over Sessions and Speakers and select All Sessions. Enter your session name in the search bar, or use the filters in the left-hand column to find it. Select your session name, then select Join as host—do not select Join as participant.

You can join as a host beginning September 28, 2026. The Join as host button will not appear before then.

I cannot find my session. What should I do?

Try searching with a shorter keyword from the title. For example, for Centering Culture for Transformative Systems Change, search for “Centering Culture.” You can also use the filters in the left-hand column, including time, date, and speaker.

Why don’t I see a “Join as host” button?

The button will not appear before September 28, 2026. If it is September 28 or later, the most likely reason is that you are not listed as a Host for that session. Please contact the Global Gathering Team at globalgathering@cuanschutz.edu so they can investigate.

Registration & Support

Do presenters need to be registered for the event?

Yes. Presenters must be registered for the Global Gathering using the email associated with their speaker profile to access Attendee Hub. For Zoom and Embedded sessions, this also enables host access. If you aren’t registered yet, register here. If your registration and speaker-profile emails differ, contact the Global Gathering Team for help.

I am not sure what email is associated with my registration. How do I find that out?

Look for your registration confirmation email with the subject line Registration Confirmed | 2026 Global Gathering for the Future of Child Welfare. You can also check an email address using the Registration Information Lookup tab.

Can my organization register as a group?

Yes. Group registration is available for organizations purchasing 10 or more registrations and offers reduced per-person rates. Many agencies, universities, nonprofits, training programs, and community partners use group registration to bring whole teams into the Global Gathering as a shared learning experience.

2026 group rates:

  • 10–19 registrants: $150/person
  • 20–29 registrants: $135/person
  • 30–39 registrants: $120/person
  • 40–49 registrants: $105/person
  • 50+ registrants: $90/person

For additional information, visit the Group Registrations page.

Who do I contact for support?

Email the Global Gathering Team at globalgathering@cuanschutz.edu. Please include your name, session title, and a short description of what you need support with so we can navigate you to the right resource.

Sign in to Attendee Hub

Identify your session

These hosting instructions apply to Zoom and Embedded sessions.

You can sign in and find your session once you’re registered. Beginning September 28, 2026, you can also join as a host to test your specific virtual session environment. To request earlier testing access, contact the Global Gathering Team.

  1. In Attendee Hub, hover over Sessions and Speakers, then select All Sessions.
  2. Search for your session title or filter by time, date, or speaker. Open your session’s page.
  3. Select Join as host, rather than Join as participant. Host access begins September 28, 2026; after that, you can enter early to prepare.
For event day: Attendees can enter five minutes before the scheduled start. Allow time to check your microphone, camera, and screen sharing before they arrive.

Become familiar with your session tools

Review these tools ahead of time, try them when host access is available, and return to these instructions when you present.

Not sure which format you have? Check the Session Information Lookup tool or compare the formats.

Opens in the Zoom app

Zoom sessions

Start from Attendee Hub, then continue in the external Zoom meeting. Download the Zoom desktop app before your session.

Know your host role & rename yourself

The first speaker to join becomes the host. Subsequent speakers and moderators become co-hosts.

Your name initially appears as Session Host. Open Participants, hover over your name, and select More → Rename.

Share content & manage participants

Use the controls at the bottom of Zoom to share video, sound, and your screen. Use Security to manage attendee permissions, including screen and video sharing.

Use polls, chat & breakout rooms

Facilitate chat, create and manage polls, and start breakout rooms as you would in a Zoom meeting. The full Speaker Guide includes links to Zoom’s instructions for these tools and downloading the desktop app.

Keep Attendee Hub handy. The session feedback survey appears on the Attendee Hub session page, not inside Zoom.
Inside Attendee Hub

Embedded sessions

Your session runs entirely in Attendee Hub. Use the controls below the video for your camera, microphone, and screen sharing.

Manage participation & recording

Attendees enter as Observers, with audio and video off. To approve someone who requests to participate, open Attendees → Requests to participate in the right engagement panel. Participants can share video, sound, and their screen.

Check your session’s recording status in Lookup. For sessions being recorded, a moderator, concierge, or speaker can select Record.

Answer live Q&A

Open Q&A on the right. In Approved, select Write answer, type your reply, and select Post response. Find and edit replies in Answered. Speakers and moderators can also answer questions verbally.

Create & launch polls

Open Polls → Create question. Choose a question type and enter your question and answer options. Select Done or Add another question.

Select Open question so attendees can see it and respond. Select Display to overlay it on the video, then Close question when finished collecting responses.

Before editing: editing a question deletes any responses already received.

Keep the chat going

Open Chat in the right toolbar. Speakers and attendees can exchange messages throughout the session. Your messages are labeled Facilitator.

Event-day reference

Invite session feedback

Remind attendees to select Survey in the right toolbar on their Attendee Hub session page. Attendees who purchased the ability to claim CEUs will see additional knowledge-check questions for CEU-eligible sessions.

A closing reminder you can read aloud
Please take a moment to complete the session feedback survey. Open our session page in Attendee Hub and select Survey in the right toolbar. If you are claiming CEUs, complete the three knowledge-check questions at the end of the form.

For Zoom sessions: explicitly direct attendees back to Attendee Hub to complete the survey.
